## Purpose
Our purpose is to transform our original Pilot Cat concept into an entertaining, interactive Unity game using the three Pipelines: Important Information, The Beginning, and Storytelling. We aim to develop Pilot Cat's 1930s-inspired world, characters, and adventures while understanding the information, planning, and creative decisions needed to bring the story to life. Through teamwork, Unity development, and C# programming, we will create a playable experience that every team member can understand, demonstrate, and explain.
